What it learns from

Two separate mechanisms. Neither one was trained on anybody else’s audio before it reached your machine.

Recorded DJ mixes experimental

Drop in a recorded set and it looks for the transitions — the bass dropping out, the top end changing over — and measures how long each one ran. That teaches it how to mix: join lengths and which kind. It cannot tell which tracks they were, so it learns nothing about track order from this.

Your own moves

Every transition that runs, every track you skip, every rating you give becomes a labelled example. That teaches it what you like: which pairings to reach for and which to avoid. This is the part that affects track order.

Any link that ends in a real audio file — your own server, a NAS, an Internet Archive item, a CC release. Streaming-service links will not work: those are DRM, not files.
